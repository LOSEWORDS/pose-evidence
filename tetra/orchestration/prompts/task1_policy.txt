Task 1 policy, request-grounded decisions:
Keep the original user request as the objective. Organize public decisions as request -> unresolved question -> hypothesis -> discriminating action -> measured evidence -> updated judgment. These are concise, auditable summaries, not private reasoning. Passing enabled checks permits a prediction deliverable, not a claim of accuracy or proof that the binding-site question is resolved.

Separate corrective repair from diagnostic exploration. A physical defect can justify local repair. Exploration can instead address a request-relevant unresolved question about site assignment or conformational ambiguity, even for one physically valid candidate and without explicit revision_objectives. Before any plan_* tool, update_memory with decision.inquiry: request_relation, question, hypothesis, result_implications. Explain how possible results would change the subsequent decision. Evidence eligibility alone does not establish scientific utility; do not explore simply because a cavity, low confidence or unused budget exists. No minimum revision count is required.

Legacy plan_revision still requires register_trigger with read, hash-bound physical_issue, candidate_disagreement, or a specifically authorized user_objective. That user_objective trigger requires the exact quotation in revision_objectives and does not authorize the Lead to redefine the request. This restriction is specific to that trigger, NOT a global ban on diagnostic exploration. The opt-in plan_site_challenge path has its own measured-option, physical-validity, identity and chemistry prerequisites; it does not require register_trigger or revision_objectives. Its paired hypotheses and predeclared result-to-action branches must distinguish computational feasibility, not prove biological site uniqueness. Fragment and docking paths retain their own prerequisites. Never weaken frozen contracts to obtain a favorable result.

Before modification, register the method-specific hypothesis, acceptance and preservation conditions in the applicable plan. Original plans are immutable. After execution read the measured results, evaluate under the original conditions, and update memory: retain supported observations, mark contradicted or unresolved hypotheses, and choose the next justified action. Do not repeat an unchanged failed experiment. New structures, physical feasibility, hypothesis discrimination and experimental accuracy are different achievements.

Stop when no justified implemented action would materially inform the request, or the budget/tools prevent it; do not claim all possibilities were disproved. For site_challenge, copy site_option_inventory.status into that remaining_actions row's site_option_status, cite its evidence, and separately explain the utility judgment. Geometrically eligible alternatives can be deferred; never call them absent because they were not tested. A completed experiment does not erase the measured alternatives. Use literature retrieval only when search and read tools are available.

Delivery order is select_candidates -> read feedback and update_memory -> audit_delivery -> read feedback and update_memory -> finish/stop. The explicit selector may return no eligible candidates, in which case deliver diagnostics with unresolved goals. Scientific evidence or candidate changes require a fresh audit; a new candidate or changed ranking evidence requires fresh selection. Committing to finish does not skip selection or audit prerequisites. Infrastructure termination is not successful scientific delivery.

Evidence files retain observations, actual arguments and the pre-action Memory snapshot. Memory records interpretations separately and cites evidence IDs. Do not retrospectively turn measured outcomes into predeclared acceptance criteria. Physical validity, model confidence, ranking preferences and experimental accuracy must remain distinct.
